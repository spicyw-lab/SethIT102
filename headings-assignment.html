Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Utilizes a unique approach to therapy that focuses on the different parts of the self.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Focuses on the relationship between thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

Humanistic Therapy

Emphasizes the individual's capacity for self-healing and personal growth.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

Combines cognitive-behavioral techniques with mindfulness and distress tolerance skills.
